# Privacy Policy for Smarana (స్మరణ)

**Effective Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 15, 2026  

Smarana ("we", "our", or "the app") is a privacy-first, 100% offline devotional companion designed for daily spiritual practice (Sadhana), including Japa, Pradakshina, Nitya Pooja, Ashtottaram, and Telugu Panchangam.

We believe that your personal prayer and spiritual practices are sacred and private. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict commitment to zero data collection.

---

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection

* **No Accounts / No Login**: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide any email, phone number, or credentials to use Smarana.
* **No Analytics or Trackers**: Smarana does **not** include any analytics SDKs (such as Google Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook Pixel). We do not track your usage, session duration, or interactions.
* **No Advertisements**: Smarana is 100% ad-free. We do not integrate any advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) and do not share any data with advertising brokers.

---

## 2. On-Device Local Storage (100% Offline)

* All your devotional settings—including your preferred language, gotram/sharma for Sankalpam, daily pooja bookmarks, and japa counts—are stored strictly **on your local device** using an encrypted local SQLite database.
* This data is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted over the internet to any external server or cloud service.

---

## 3. Device Sensors & Permissions

Smarana requests only the minimal permissions required to facilitate authentic spiritual sadhana:

* **Physical Sensors (Accelerometer / Gyroscope)**:
  * Used solely while you actively perform a **Pradakshina** or **Japa** session to detect deliberate steps (auto-loop) or back-of-phone double-tap gestures.
  * Sensor samples are processed in real-time in device memory and immediately discarded. No sensor logs or movement data are ever recorded or transmitted.
* **Vibration (Haptics)**:
  * Used to provide silent physical feedback when you complete a bead count, Meru bead boundary, or pradakshina round without looking at the screen.
* **Notifications**:
  * Used exclusively for optional, user-scheduled local reminders (such as the daily 6:00 AM Panchangam reminder or sadhana completion). All notifications are generated locally on your device without push servers.
* **Foreground Service**:
  * Used strictly during an active Pocket Japa or Pradakshina countdown session to detect back-of-phone taps while the device is in your pocket or when the screen is locked, ensuring seamless counting.

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing (WhatsApp 1-Tap Share)

Smarana includes an optional 1-tap button to share daily Panchangam information via WhatsApp. 
* This action uses standard Android system intents (`whatsapp://send`).
* Smarana does not read your WhatsApp contacts, messages, or account information. You maintain full control over which contacts or groups you send the message to.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Smarana does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults of any age. The app is completely safe for all audiences and age groups.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Because Smarana does not collect user data, we do not anticipate material changes to this policy. Any minor updates will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date in the app repository.
